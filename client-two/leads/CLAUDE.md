# Rules for the leads folder

- These are made-up practice people. They are not real customers.
- Never add real people or real email addresses to any file in this folder.
- Every row needs a consent value. No consent, no row.
- Never change the @example.com addresses. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail.

## Groups
- **weekend-bakers** – people who love baking at home on weekends and want recipes and ideas.
- **coffee-lovers** – people who pop in for coffee and want something good to go with it.
