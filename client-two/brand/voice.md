# Rise & Crumb – brand voice

## Our voice in three words
Warm, cheerful, homely.

## Words we use
fresh, golden, crusty, bake, oven, weekend, share

## Words we avoid
healthy, guilt-free, superfood, detox, last chance, hurry

## Claims we must never make
No health claims (healthy, low-calorie, good for your gut). No fake urgency. No prices we haven't confirmed.

## How we sign off
Fresh from the oven,
The Rise & Crumb team
